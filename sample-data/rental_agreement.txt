RENTAL AGREEMENT
This rental agreement is made between the landlord and the tenant.
The monthly rent is Rs 25000 payable on the first of each month.
The security deposit is Rs 150000, equal to six months rent.
The lease duration is 11 months with a lock-in period of 9 months.
The landlord may terminate this agreement at any time by giving 15 days notice.
Rent shall be increased by the landlord every year at the landlord's discretion.
