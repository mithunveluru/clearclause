PERSONAL LOAN AGREEMENT
A processing fee of 2 percent of the sanctioned amount shall be deducted at the time of disbursal.
A late payment charge of Rs 500 per month will be levied on any overdue installment.
Foreclosure of the loan before 12 months will attract a charge of 4 percent on the outstanding principal.
Charges and interest rates may be revised at the sole discretion of the company without prior notice.
Other charges may apply as determined by the lender from time to time.
